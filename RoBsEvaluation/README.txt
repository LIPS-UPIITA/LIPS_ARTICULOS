This folder contains files documenting the summarized results of the bias assessment for the systematic review of the 12 articles under analysis.

1. The folder contains a file named "Evaluación de sesgo.pdf," which outlines the recommended assessment approach based on study type, information sources, and keywords to search for within the document.

2. The "GUIAS_METODOSRoBS" folder contains practical guides for applying the method.

3. Twelve folders labeled "IDX_Source_NUM" contain the 12 articles and the assessment report, which includes the findings and justifications from two reviewers, as well as the consensus decision supporting the information in Appendix C.


Reported in "Electrophysiological signatures of sarcopenia: a systematic
review of sEMG features, fatigue indices and AI based classifiers", journal Version July 30, 2026 submitted to Sensors, MDPI
